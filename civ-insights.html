Sept 22 Patch — Full Changelog

Everything this site's Current/Sept 22 toggle (on index.html and build-order.html) currently models for The Viking Sagas DLC's balance patch, organized general changes first, then by civ.

General

  • Eagle Scout train time: 50s → 46s (Eagle Warrior/Elite unchanged).
  • Battering Ram line (all tiers): Line of Sight 3 → 5.
  • Siege Tower: Line of Sight 8 → 6.
  • Onager (the Imperial-age Mangonel-line tier): attack 50 → 55, pierce armor 7 → 8.
  • Mounted Crossbowman — new Archery Range unit (Castle Age): 40w 65g cost, 45 HP, 7 attack, 2 melee/0 pierce armor, 4 range, 1.25 speed, 37s train time. Heavy Mounted Crossbowman (Imperial): 8 attack, upgrade cost 1000f 650g. See "By Civ" below for who gets it (it replaces Cavalry Archer for those civs).
  • Varangian Guard — new Barracks unique unit (Castle Age), shared by Byzantines and Vikings (Danes/Saxons/Varangians already have it as a core civ unit): 65f 45g cost, 30s train time, 70 HP, 13 attack, 2 melee/1 pierce armor, 1.05 speed.
  • Cranequins — new Imperial-age upgrade tech for Mounted Crossbowman: 300f 200g, +1 range, +2 attack vs. Infantry. Only some Mounted-Crossbowman civs get it (see below).
  • Ordonnance Companies — new Castle-age tech, Franks-exclusive: Mounted Crossbowmen cost -40% gold.

Naval & Buildings

  • Demolition Ship upgrade cost: 50w 100g, 50s research → 25w 75g, 20s research.
  • Hulk line (all tiers): melee armor 4/5/6 → 3/4/5.
  • Hulk: HP 90 → 85.
  • War Hulk: HP 115 → 110.
  • Lou Chuan — regional naval unit: bonus damage vs. Buildings +230 → +180.
  • Fire Galley: bonus damage vs. Buildings 1 → 0.
  • Fortified Church (Armenians & Georgians only): build time 40s → 50s.

Real finding, with both sides at full tech completion: melee armor now TIES at 5 (Parthian Tactics' own melee-armor point exactly closes the gap Mounted Crossbowman's higher base melee armor used to hold alone). Mounted Crossbowman still wins range (8 vs. 7 — Cranequins' own +1 range stacks on the shared Fletching/Bodkin Arrow/Bracer line, where Parthian Tactics spends its equivalent "extra" on pierce armor instead) and flat attack (12 vs. 11 — entirely the base units' own 8-vs-7 attack difference, since every tech bonus applied is identical between them). Cavalry Archer wins on HP (+15 — Bloodlines is the same flat +20 for both lines now, so this gap is entirely the base units' own 60-vs-45 HP difference), pierce armor (+2 — Parthian Tactics' own pierce bonus is 1 point ahead of Cranequins' equivalent), speed (1.61 vs. 1.44 — same +15% Husbandry bonus on both sides, so the gap is just the base 1.4-vs-1.25 speed carried through), AND despite the lower flat attack, DPS too (6.11 vs. 5.33 for Danes/Saxons, 6.11 vs. 4.8 for Varangians) — its faster base 2s reload (vs. 2.5s) carries through proportionally even after both sides get the same -10% Thumb Ring reduction. Each side's own situational vs.-class bonus (+2, applying to a different target class each) keeps their real combat attack close either way.

Starting from Reached Feudal Age, this compares two full-commitment strategies from that point on: keep building fresh Lumber Camps purely for the bonus (nobody ever farms), vs. abandon that and build real farms instead (60 wood, 175 food capacity each, real depletion and rebuild). Computed live against this site's own Optimize-mode gather rates and build costs — not a fixed snapshot.
