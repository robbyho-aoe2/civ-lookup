Sept 22 Patch — Full Changelog

Everything this site's Current/Sept 22 toggle (on index.html and build-order.html) currently models for The Viking Sagas DLC's balance patch, organized general changes first, then by civ.

General

  • Eagle Scout train time: 50s → 46s (Eagle Warrior/Elite unchanged).
  • Battering Ram line (all tiers): Line of Sight 3 → 5.
  • Siege Tower: Line of Sight 8 → 6.
  • Onager (the Imperial-age Mangonel-line tier): attack 50 → 55, pierce armor 7 → 8.
  • Mounted Crossbowman — new Archery Range unit (Castle Age): 40w 65g cost, 45 HP, 7 attack, 2 melee/0 pierce armor, 4 range, 1.25 speed, 40s train time. Heavy Mounted Crossbowman (Imperial): 8 attack, 30s train time, upgrade cost 1000f 650g. See "By Civ" below for who gets it (it replaces Cavalry Archer for those civs).
  • Varangian Guard — new Barracks unique unit (Castle Age), shared by Byzantines and Vikings (Danes/Saxons/Varangians already have it as a core civ unit): 65f 45g cost, 30s train time, 70 HP, 13 attack, 2 melee/1 pierce armor, 1.05 speed.
  • Cranequins — new Imperial-age upgrade tech for Mounted Crossbowman: 300f 200g, +1 range, +2 attack vs. Infantry. Only some Mounted-Crossbowman civs get it (see below).
  • Ordonnance Companies — new Castle-age tech, Franks-exclusive: Mounted Crossbowmen cost -40% gold.

Naval & Buildings

  • Demolition Ship upgrade cost: 50w 100g, 50s research → 25w 75g, 20s research.
  • Hulk line (all tiers): melee armor 4/5/6 → 3/4/5.
  • Hulk: HP 90 → 85.
  • War Hulk: HP 115 → 110.
  • Lou Chuan — regional naval unit: bonus damage vs. Buildings +230 → +180.
  • Fire Galley: bonus damage vs. Buildings 1 → 0.
  • Fortified Church (Armenians & Georgians only): build time 40s → 50s.

Saxons: Lumber Camps vs. Farms

Saxons' Mills, Lumber Camps, and Mining Camps grant bonus food and +10 stone the instant they're built — +35 confirmed for the Sept 22 patch, though the original DLC preview announced +50 (toggle below to compare either). Starting from Reached Feudal Age, this compares two full-commitment strategies from that point on: keep building fresh Lumber Camps purely for the bonus (nobody ever farms), vs. abandon that and build real farms instead (60 wood, 175 food capacity each, real depletion and rebuild). Computed live against this site's own Optimize-mode gather rates and build costs — not a fixed snapshot. The only bottleneck on camp count is real wood income: a new camp starts the instant 100 wood is banked and its builder comes straight back to gathering once it's done (no artificial cap on how many go up at once) — so the camp curve below is exactly what that population's wood-gathering rate can fund, not an assumed space or travel-time limit.
