Saxons: Lumber Camps vs. Farms

Saxons' Mills, Lumber Camps, and Mining Camps grant +50 food and +10 stone the instant they're built. Starting from Reached Feudal Age, this compares two full-commitment strategies from that point on: keep building fresh Lumber Camps purely for the bonus (nobody ever farms), vs. abandon that and build real farms instead (60 wood, 175 food capacity each, real depletion and rebuild). Computed live against this site's own Optimize-mode gather rates and build costs — not a fixed snapshot. The only bottleneck on camp count is real wood income: a new camp starts the instant 100 wood is banked and its builder comes straight back to gathering once it's done (no artificial cap on how many go up at once) — so the camp curve below is exactly what that population's wood-gathering rate can fund, not an assumed space or travel-time limit.
