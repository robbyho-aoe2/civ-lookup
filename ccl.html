Rules

How the league works.

  1. Sign-ups. Post “IN” by 12 PM (EST) on game day. You may request teammates, but final teams and formats are the organizer's call. No-shows = −1 pt.
  2. Formats. Weekly formats vary (FFA, team games, multi-team, experimental). They are announced before the games.
  3. Participation. Play to completion or be eliminated legitimately to earn points. Disconnects are handled by the organizer. Minimum 1 pt per game.
  4. Voice chat. Players must stay in their team VC. Non-players are not allowed. Polite channel hopping is ok.
  5. Points. 1 pt minimum. Placement points scale with the number of players or teams. Example: in an 8-player FFA, 1st = 8 pts, … last = 1 pt. In team games, all team members get the same points.
  6. Multiple games. Each game awards points independently, and the leaderboards track season totals.
  7. Conduct. No griefing, FFA teaming, match-fixing or stream sniping. Trash talk is ok if respectful. Organizer rulings are final.
  8. Admin and caster. NotATheist
  9. Seasons. 12-week seasons. Points reset each season. Standings are for bragging rights, titles and possible prizes.
