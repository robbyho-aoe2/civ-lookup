The question isn't "how often is each candidate quiet" — it's whether they go MORE quiet specifically when Garyy faces tough opponents, compared to how often they're normally quiet at that exact time of day on random other days — no reference to Garyy at all.

What's "baseline"? How often this person is normally quiet at that same clock time, on random days that have nothing to do with Garyy — just their everyday schedule. If their quiet rate during Garyy's tough games is way above that, it's not just a quiet moment, it's happening specifically when it matters. The p-value is the odds that gap would show up by pure chance.

A CLEANER TEST

The easy-vs-tough comparison has a flaw: if a candidate is also playing some of Garyy's easy games, the "easy" bucket stops being a clean baseline — it's still built from Garyy's own account. Fix: compare both buckets instead to how often each candidate is normally quiet at that exact clock time, on random days that have nothing to do with Garyy at all.

Tough games, both candidates

p < 0.0001 — essentially impossible to reach by chance, even measured against each candidate's own normal behavior on days with no connection to Garyy. Whatever's driving this, it isn't noise.

Easy games, the two diverge

BillieDown is unusually active (p=0.0006) — his normal schedule overlapping Garyy's, not evidence of involvement. ItsGuts shows no significant shift either way (p=0.185).

The Case, In Brief

TWO ACCOUNTS GO QUIET
EXACTLY WHEN IT MATTERS

< 1 in 10,000

chance either is coincidence (p < 0.0001 each), measured against each account's own normal behavior on days with no connection to Garyy. Of his 17 toughest games since crossing 1700 rating, both BillieDown and ItsGuts group were quiet for all 17 — zero exceptions, each.

Not a habit

At that exact time-of-day one and two weeks earlier, both were often still active — BillieDown 82%/53% of the time, ItsGuts 77%/65%. The quiet tracks the real games, not a standing weekly gap.

Mid-session, then gone

On the days this happens, both are often deep in their own sessions right up until the tough game starts, then resume right after — not days they're simply absent.

Caveat: this pattern is specific to Garyy's hardest matchups. It isn't a claim that one person plays the whole account, or that BillieDown and ItsGuts cover every tough game between them — lower-elo games, and some of the other high-elo games, may well be Garyy himself or other players in his circle. A handful of tough games (the 3-6am stretch) can't be attributed either way with this method.
