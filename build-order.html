What this does

Pick a civ, the economy upgrades you'll have, and the army you want to keep producing — this works out the resource income you need and tells you how many villagers to put on Wood/Food/Gold/Stone to sustain it.

Steady-state estimate, not a minute-by-minute timeline: it assumes you keep this mix in constant production forever (not a one-time batch) and food comes from farms (not hunting/berries). Villager counts are a target ratio to sustain that rotation, not "move vills at pop X" instructions. The Wood total includes farm upkeep (60 wood to rebuild a depleted farm) and house upkeep (25 wood per House, 5 pop each) — food and population both need wood villagers behind them, not just their own gather rate.

Army Composition

Set how many production buildings you'll keep queuing each unit — 2 Barracks on Long Swordsmen, 1 Archery Range on Crossbowmen, etc. Zero = not in production. Pick each line's current upgrade tier — training cost doesn't change, but train time does. The Town Center row's Villager count defaults to 1 (constant villager production) — set it to 0 for "all-in" (stop making villagers). Goal Qty is separate: a one-time batch (e.g. 10 Man-at-Arms) to add as a target in the goals panel below, on top of whatever's ongoing.
