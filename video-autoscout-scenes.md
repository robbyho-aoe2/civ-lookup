# Video: "10 things you need to see on aoe2autoscout.com"

Draft scene plan, v2. Countdown format: 10 is the least useful or interesting, 1 is the most. Screen-recorded walkthrough, voice-over recorded afterwards.
Audience: console players first, anyone welcome. Target length about 10-12 minutes. Durations are estimates, trim on the timeline.
The lower numbers run shorter (about 35-45 s) and the top of the countdown runs longer (60-90 s), so the video builds.

so the Saturday Night Live report moved up from the honorable mentions to number 8 to keep it a top ten. Swap it for anything you prefer.

## Before recording
- Record the site at 1920x1080, browser zoom 110-125% so text reads on a phone screen.
- Record a short phone-width pass too (console players watch on phones). Scenes marked PHONE have a second take.
- Pick demo accounts once and reuse them so the video feels like one story. Suggested: one top console player,
  one mid-rated viewer-style account, one confirmed alt pair. Confirm with the player first if it is not you.
- Clear the Console Live filter and any followed players before the number 1 scene.
- Have one finished game from a few hours ago ready for numbers 5 and 4, so the recording and build order are fresh.
- Capture one clip on a PC of AoE2 opening a recording or spectating (number 5 needs it).
- For number 3, run the Optimize setups first (Generic Knight, a civ pick, the two-stage Scout Cavalry then Knights, all-in on)
  and note each ready time, so the voice-over quotes real numbers.

## 3. Build Order Optimizer (100 s)
- Page: build-order.html, the Optimize tool. Open with the "Choose a Tool" card on screen for 5 s so viewers see the three tools
  (Constant Production, Optimize, Player Builds), then click Optimize.
- Beat 1, the one-click answer (25 s): leave Generic selected, pick Knight as the target, no other settings.
  The result line reads "Fastest path to 1x Knight in constant production, ready by 17:28, using 1 Town Center".
  Scroll the Villager Allocation card: villager split (15 food, 2 wood, 10 gold), then the Step-by-Step table:
  Lumber Camp at 1:58, Mill at 3:26, Loom, Feudal at 6:59, Mining Camp 9:39, Stable and Blacksmith 13:29, Castle 14:19.
  Check the numbers again on the day, they change with the data.
  Callouts: "Pick a unit, get the fastest path", "It decides the upgrades and extra Town Centers for you".
- Beat 2, make it yours (25 s): choose a civ (try one with an economy bonus), watch the split change.
  Add a stage: Scout Cavalry in Feudal, then Knights in Castle. Say what you changed, then show the new ready time.
  Run it before recording and write the new time on a card so the voice-over matches.
  Callouts: "Add stages to plan a transition", "Civ bonuses applied".
- Beat 3, the controls (25 s): Efficiency (85% by default, "real play is never 100%"), Research Loom, and Prioritize Military (all-in),
  then the hunt options: Map Has deer or chickens, Deer Strategy, Spawn Size. Flip all-in on and show how the timing moves.
  Callouts: "Efficiency = how clean your play is", "All-in skips the safe eco and spends everything on the unit".
- Beat 4, the other two tools (20 s, quick): Constant Production (tick what you have and what you want, see the ideal villager split),
  then Player Builds (build orders worked out from real players' replays). One shot and one line each.
  Callouts: "Plan it", "Copy a real player's", "Optimize it".
- Voice beats: stop guessing your first minutes; this is the plan on paper before you queue; try the same unit with efficiency at 70
  and see what slop costs you. End on a challenge: "post your best Knight time in the comments".
- Be honest on camera: it is an idealised model (instant spending, constant villager production), so it gives the target, not a
  guarantee. The page says the same. Do not promise "pro timings".
- PHONE take of beat 1 only: the Step-by-Step table is wide, so record it scrolled sideways, or crop to the first columns.
